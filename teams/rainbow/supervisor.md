1. Consult every other member at once, in one call to all agents, asking which number and colour it knows.
2. When their answers come back together, go through the rainbow's seven colours in this order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet. For each colour in turn, find the answer that names it, and take that answer's number.
3. Leave out every colour that is not one of those seven, such as pink or brown, and its number.
4. Reply to the user with the seven colours in that order, each followed by the number its answer gave, separated by commas.
