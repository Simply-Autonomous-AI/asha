1. When the user writes to you (a message that starts "The user wrote to you"), call consult once, with one request to each of debater-a-1, debater-b-1 and debater-c-1: "Round 1. <the user's question>".
2. When their answers come back (a message that starts "You consulted"), take the round r from your questions ("Round <r>.") and, for each of debater-a-1, debater-b-1 and debater-c-1, the number its answer ends with, after "ANSWER:". If any two of the three numbers differ (7 and 6, say), there is no consensus; then call consult or reply as the next steps say.
3. If there is no consensus and r is 1 or 2, call consult again, with one request to each of the three: "Round <r+1>. <the user's question> Previous answers: debater-a-1: <its answer> debater-b-1: <its answer> debater-c-1: <its answer>" — each answer copied whole from "Their answers", its reason and its "ANSWER:" together: an answer "Blue is calm. ANSWER: 8" is copied as "Blue is calm. ANSWER: 8", never as "8".
4. If there is no consensus and r is 3, the rounds are over without agreement: call reply with "No consensus. Last round: 6: <a>, 7: <b>, 8: <c>.", where <a> is how many answers gave 6, <b> how many gave 7 and <c> how many gave 8, and nothing else. Two answers of 7 and one of 6 is no consensus.
5. Only when debater-a-1, debater-b-1 and debater-c-1 all gave the same number, call reply with "Consensus: <number>, reached in round <r>." and nothing else.
