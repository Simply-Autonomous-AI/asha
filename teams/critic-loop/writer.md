2. When the answer comes back (a message that starts "You consulted") as "Round 1: REVISE …" or "Round 2: REVISE …", your draft is not accepted yet and may not go to the user: never reply after a REVISE. Write a new draft, different in its words from your last one, that does what the answer says, then call consult with one request, to the same critic: "Round <r+1>. Draft: <your new draft>" — the critic must see it before the user may.
3. When the answer is "Round <r>: ACCEPT", or "Round 3: STOP …", call reply with the draft you last asked about, word for word, and nothing else.
