What it is for

Use it when a decision benefits from the strongest case on each side before someone decides.

Do not use it when the participants should converge on their own (consensus, P23), or when a count is enough (voting, P10).

How the messages flow

Run it

Launch the team, then send a scenario's message: exactly the commands below, which the demo's agent-suite file reads from this page and runs. The # bound line is the most a request may take (docs/plans/orchestration-patterns.md R8): 34,000 ms, set from its first three recorded runs (docs/plans/patterns-phase-2.md R4) — the largest request, p09-a's 22,446 ms at 1303318, times 1.5 (33,669), rounded up to a whole second; the cap is 45,000 ms.

The team and the test

  • teams/debate/team.json: the team — judge, optimist, sceptic, launched in that order, each joining as <name>-1.
  • judge: judge.json, its recipe, and judge.md, its guidance, a full-text document in its system prompt.
  • optimist: optimist.json, its recipe, and optimist.md, its guidance, a full-text document in its system prompt.
  • sceptic: sceptic.json, its recipe, and sceptic.md, its guidance, a full-text document in its system prompt.
  • tests/agents/p09-debate.test.ts: launches the team and sends each scenario's message from this page, then asserts each scenario only through its checker, P09_A in tests/agents/patterns/phase-2.ts, and records the checker's proof for the report.
  • The checker's fixtures, one passing conversation, one per mutation the plan lists and one breaking every other claim: tests/agents/patterns/fixtures/p09-a/, run by tests/unit/pattern-proofs.test.ts in npm test.

Limits on a local model

  • Measured in the premise probes (2026-09-29, 0be190f), its chain of model turns took 17.6 s with cold prompts and 9.0 s warm (docs/plans/patterns-phase-2.md).
  • Round 2's question must quote both round-1 answers word for word, stance lines included; a judge that paraphrases fails the proof.
