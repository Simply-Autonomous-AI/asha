What it is for

Use it when several agents could do a task and the choice between them depends on what each offers now.

Do not use it when the choice is fixed by role (router, P2) or when every agent should do its part (orchestrator–workers, P5).

The team and the test

  • teams/contract-net/team.json: the team — manager, courier-north, courier-south, courier-east, launched in that order, each joining as <name>-1.
  • manager: manager.json, its recipe, and manager.md, its guidance, a full-text document in its system prompt.
  • courier-north: courier-north.json, its recipe, and courier-north.md, its guidance, a full-text document in its system prompt.
  • courier-south: courier-south.json, its recipe, and courier-south.md, its guidance, a full-text document in its system prompt.
  • courier-east: courier-east.json, its recipe, and courier-east.md, its guidance, a full-text document in its system prompt.
  • tests/agents/p13-contract-net.test.ts: launches the team and sends each scenario's message from this page, then asserts each scenario only through its checker, P13_A in tests/agents/patterns/phase-2.ts, and records the checker's proof for the report.
  • The checker's fixtures, one passing conversation, one per mutation the plan lists and one breaking every other claim: tests/agents/patterns/fixtures/p13-a/, run by tests/unit/pattern-proofs.test.ts in npm test.
