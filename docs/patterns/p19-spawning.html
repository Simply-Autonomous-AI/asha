P19 · Dynamic spawning of workers

A lead brings a new worker onto the channel when it needs one, and calls it in the same turn.

What it is for

Use it when the agents a request needs are not known until it arrives, or are too many or too costly to keep running: the lead brings them in for one conversation, and they go when it ends.

Do not use it when the same workers serve every request (keep them on the channel: orchestrator–workers, P5), or when the worker must keep what it learned past the conversation (a launched agent persists nothing).

The team and the test

  • teams/spawner/team.json: the team — lead alone, joining as lead-1.
  • lead: lead.json, its recipe, with its launch grant and a messageTimeoutMs of 120,000 (the launch lies inside its turn), and lead.md, its guidance, a full-text document in its system prompt.
  • helper (launched, no member): helper.json, with a startupTimeoutMs of 30,000, and helper.md.
  • tests/agents/p19-spawning.test.ts: launches the team and sends the scenario's message from this page, then asserts it only through its checker, P19_A in tests/agents/patterns/phase-3.ts, and records the checker's proof for the report.
  • The checker's fixtures, one passing conversation, one per mutation the plan lists and one breaking every other claim: tests/agents/patterns/fixtures/p19-a/, run by tests/unit/pattern-proofs.test.ts in npm test.

Limits on a local model

  • A launch takes about half a second warm (the image built and the model serving already: the launched recipe must share the lead's image and model), and lies inside the lead's turn, bounded at 35,000 ms; a helper that does not come online in its startupTimeoutMs (at most 30,000) is launch_failed, which the model is told.
  • One helper per conversation (maxPerConversation): a second launch is refused launch_cap, which the model is told. The route's refusals and the helper's stop are proven without a model in daemon.test.ts ("an agent launching from its grant").
  • Without its grant the lead has no tool to launch with, and does not (the plan's control C3, 0/3).
